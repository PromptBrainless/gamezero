# Visual Reference Brief

The [Sprint 1 production pack](art-sprint-1-production-pack.md) is the detailed source of truth for the current HUD/icon art sprint. This brief records only the stable visual direction; character, region, and boss art remain outside Sprint 1.

## Principles

- Tactical clarity and readable silhouette before detail.
- Restrained, grounded fantasy materials and authored shapes; avoid noisy ornament or generic sci-fi interface styling.
- Combat backgrounds and decoration must preserve contrast for units, targets, and HUD values.
- Use the palette and shape cues in the Sprint 1 pack; color is never the only semantic distinction.

## Prompt structure

For later visual work, specify subject and purpose, silhouette, material, palette, lighting, camera/framing, background, and negative constraints. Request one primary subject per asset and keep labels/numbers outside generated art.

Useful exclusions include text, logos, watermarks, modern objects, illegible symbols, excess gore, tiny detail, low contrast, and mechanics or lore not yet approved.

## Handoff

Use the format, naming, source-file, and QA rules in the relevant production pack. Do not assume the character/background/FX sizes or locations proposed by earlier PRs are final for the target runtime.
